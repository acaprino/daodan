# Agent role boundaries, tool use, feedback, independent verification and evaluation

## What do ReAct, Reflexion, Self-Refine, Large Language Models Cannot Self-Correct Reasoning Yet, Chain-of-Verification, MetaGPT, AutoGen and AgentBench show?

### Takeaway

The strongest reusable boundary is not “one agent per persona”. It is the separation of proposal, environment interaction, evidence and acceptance. Use a bounded ReAct loop for tool work; add reflection only when a reliable feedback signal exists; do not treat self-critique, debate or role proliferation as correctness mechanisms.

### Cited Findings

- **ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models** (ICLR 2023), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629), [official project](https://react-lm.github.io/). **Evidence type:** controlled prompting and fine-tuning experiments on HotpotQA, FEVER, ALFWorld and WebShop, plus trajectory and failure analysis. ReAct interleaves reasoning traces with actions and observations. On ALFWorld and WebShop it reported absolute success-rate gains of 34% and 10% over imitation or reinforcement-learning baselines, with one or two in-context examples; on knowledge tasks, Wikipedia observations reduced some hallucination and error propagation. **Limitations:** results are benchmark and prompt specific; ReAct can still repeat hallucinated actions or misread observations, and the paper does not establish a universal cost advantage. **Default: ADOPT** a bounded thought/action/observation loop where tools supply needed state; do not expose private chain-of-thought as a requirement. Store concise action rationales, tool arguments, observations and outcomes instead. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2210.03629)

- **Reflexion: Language Agents with Verbal Reinforcement Learning** (NeurIPS 2023), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.11366), [official code](https://github.com/noahshinn024/reflexion). **Evidence type:** experiments and ablations on ALFWorld, HotpotQA and programming tasks. Reflexion converts scalar, binary, heuristic or model-generated feedback into verbal reflections kept in episodic memory for later trials. The paper reports absolute improvements of 22% on ALFWorld, 20% on HotpotQA and up to 11% on Python programming over its baselines, and a benchmark-specific 91% HumanEval result. **Limitations:** feedback quality and credit assignment are bottlenecks; on WebShop the agent did not improve after four trials and produced unhelpful reflections, showing difficulty with exploration and local minima. These are not universal conversion factors for other models or tasks. **Default: TEST** only with observable feedback, a retry cap and a comparison against simply spending the same budget on fresh attempts. **AVOID** unconstrained reflection loops. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2303.11366)

- **Self-Refine: Iterative Refinement with Self-Feedback** (NeurIPS 2023), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.17651), [official project](https://selfrefine.info/). **Evidence type:** seven generation tasks evaluated with human preferences and automatic metrics, using GPT-3.5 and GPT-4. The same model drafts, supplies actionable feedback and revises until a stopping condition. The paper reports roughly 20% absolute average improvement over one-step generation across its tasks. **Limitations:** English-only experiments, dependence on the base model’s feedback quality, possible non-monotonic quality across iterations, and no general guarantee of factual or reasoning correctness. The paper also notes no explicit defense against harmful refinement. **Default: TEST** for editable generation with a task-specific rubric, best-of revision selection and a hard iteration cap. **AVOID** treating the pattern as proof that intrinsic reasoning errors will be corrected. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2303.17651)

- **Large Language Models Cannot Self-Correct Reasoning Yet** (ICLR 2024), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.01798). **Evidence type:** controlled reasoning experiments that remove oracle labels, equalize the number of model responses and repair unequal prompts. The central result is negative: intrinsic self-correction, defined as correction without external feedback, often failed and sometimes reduced accuracy. Improvements attributed to self-correction disappeared when oracle correctness labels were removed. Multi-agent debate did not outperform self-consistency at an equivalent number of responses. Some apparent gains came from giving the revision prompt better task information than the initial prompt. **Limitations:** the title is deliberately broad, but the experiments concern particular reasoning tasks, models and prompts; the paper does not prove that every form of revision, external feedback or tool-assisted correction fails. **Default: AVOID** intrinsic self-correction as a stand-alone acceptance gate; **ADOPT** its oracle-free, prompt-matched and cost-matched controls. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.01798)

- **Chain-of-Verification Reduces Hallucination in Large Language Models** (2023), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.11495). **Evidence type:** experiments on Wikidata list questions, Wiki-Category List, MultiSpanQA and generated biographies. CoVe drafts an answer, plans verification questions, answers them independently and produces a revised answer. Factored and two-step variants reduce attention to the original draft, which matters because models can repeat hallucinations already present in context. Short, open verification questions performed better than relying on the original long-form generation. **Limitations:** CoVe reduced but did not eliminate hallucinations; the work tested direct factual inaccuracies rather than every reasoning failure; verification remains bounded by what the model knows. It used no external retrieval and explicitly reports greater computational expense from additional output tokens. **Default: TEST** as structured internal preflight for low-risk factual drafting. **AVOID** presenting its self-generated checks as external evidence or citations. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.11495)

- **MetaGPT: Meta Programming for A Multi-Agent Collaborative Framework** (ICLR 2024; revised paper 2024), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.00352), [official project](https://github.com/FoundationAgents/MetaGPT). **Evidence type:** collaborative software-engineering benchmarks, qualitative artifacts and ablations of roles and executable feedback. MetaGPT encodes Standard Operating Procedures, assigns specialized roles and requires structured intermediate artifacts such as requirements, designs and interface specifications. The design targets cascading hallucinations and incoherent free-form chat; executable feedback is an explicit mechanism rather than another conversational opinion. **Limitations:** software-task results do not establish benefits for arbitrary domains; role prompts and artifacts do not themselves prove correctness. Multiple roles add calls, context and coordination paths, while the reported task-completion results do not provide a general token multiplier. **Default: ADOPT** explicit ownership, typed artifacts and handoff contracts; **TEST** the full multi-agent pipeline only when decomposition is independently useful. **AVOID** equating more roles with more independence. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.00352)

- **AutoGen: Enabling Next-Gen LLM Applications via Multi-Agent Conversation** (2023; revised 2024), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08155), [official project](https://github.com/microsoft/autogen). **Evidence type:** framework paper with example applications and ablations across mathematics, coding, question answering, retrieval, operations research and online decision-making. Its useful abstraction is conversation programming: customizable agents may combine LLMs, tools and human input, with configurable static or dynamic interaction patterns. It supports distinct proposer, executor, feedback and human-approval functions. **Limitations:** heterogeneous application demonstrations are not a controlled proof that multi-agent conversation is generally superior; flexibility makes orchestration, termination, permissions and evaluation the application owner’s responsibility. **Default: WATCH** as general evidence for superiority; **TEST** as an implementation framework only when its conversation and human/tool integration features fit the need. **ADOPT** explicit termination and human checkpoints for consequential actions. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.08155)

- **AgentBench: Evaluating LLMs as Agents** (ICLR 2024), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.03688), [official project](https://github.com/THUDM/AgentBench). **Evidence type:** multi-environment benchmark of 29 API-based and open-source models across eight environments: operating system, database, knowledge graph, digital card game, lateral-thinking puzzles, household tasks, web shopping and web browsing. Top commercial models outperformed many open-source competitors in that study. Failure analysis identified long-horizon reasoning, decision-making and instruction following as major obstacles; “task limit exceeded” was a predominant failure cause. Code training had mixed effects across tasks. **Limitations:** simulated, text-only environments and a model snapshot do not measure every real UI, production integration or current model; an aggregate score can hide environment-specific failure. **Default: ADOPT** its multi-environment, failure-category and trajectory-level evaluation mindset; **WATCH** leaderboard numbers as dated evidence rather than deployment acceptance. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.03688)

- A minimal operational architecture follows from the contrast among these papers: a **coordinator** owns scope, budget and stop conditions; an **actor** proposes actions; **tools** return observable state; a **verifier** checks independent evidence; an **adjudicator** resolves failed or conflicting checks; a **reporter** separates observed, inferred and unverified claims. One process may hold several roles, but the evidence source and acceptance authority must remain explicit. This is an architectural inference from ReAct, Reflexion, MetaGPT and AutoGen, not a benchmarked universal topology. [Sources](https://arxiv.org/html/2210.03629), [source](https://arxiv.org/html/2303.11366), [source](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.00352), [source](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.08155)

### Inferences

- Make tool contracts narrower than agent prompts: explicit schemas, permissions, timeouts, retries and idempotency rules make ReAct observations usable as evidence. A polished rationale cannot compensate for an unobserved or failed action. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2210.03629)
- Separate “the producer agrees with itself” from “an independent check passed”. A second persona using the same context and model is not independent evidence. Independence can come from execution, external data, a held-out human decision, or at least a separately conditioned verification pass, in increasing order of evidentiary strength depending on the claim. [Sources](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.01798), [source](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.11495)
- Give each role a delivery and a prohibition. Example: implementer delivers a patch but cannot declare acceptance; verifier delivers reproducible check results but does not silently rewrite the candidate; coordinator can stop for budget or missing evidence. This realizes MetaGPT’s artifact boundary without requiring many agents. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.00352)
- Define terminal states before execution: accepted, rejected with evidence, blocked awaiting input, budget exhausted, or unsafe/action denied. AgentBench’s dominant limit-exceeded failures and AutoGen’s configurable conversation patterns make termination a first-class contract. [Sources](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.03688), [source](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.08155)

### Gaps

- The cited architecture papers do not provide a universal token or dollar overhead for ReAct, MetaGPT or AutoGen. Actual overhead depends on trajectory length, retries, context reuse, model prices, tool latency and termination policy.
- AutoGen and MetaGPT do not, in these papers, isolate every contribution of role specialization from prompt quality, tool access and extra inference. Do not infer that a role roster alone causes improvements.
- AgentBench does not establish production reliability, security or UI accessibility. Those require target-system tests and runtime evidence.

## When does external feedback or independent verification beat intrinsic self-correction, and what is the token/orchestration cost?

### Takeaway

External feedback has the clearest advantage when it changes the evidence available to the system: tests execute, a tool observes state, a source supports a fact, or a qualified human resolves ambiguity. Independent verification is most valuable when its failure modes are less correlated with the producer’s. Every added round must justify itself against a simpler baseline at the same token, query, dollar and latency budget.

### Cited Findings

- **Prefer external signals for objectively checkable work.** Reflexion supports environment rewards, heuristics, compilers and tests; ReAct supplies fresh observations; the self-correction critique shows that gains based on oracle labels do not demonstrate intrinsic correction. For coding, a failing test is evidence about behavior, while “the code looks correct” is another model judgment. For factual work, retrieved evidence can add information unavailable in model weights. [Sources](https://arxiv.org/html/2303.11366), [source](https://arxiv.org/html/2210.03629), [source](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.01798), [source](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.11495)

- **External does not automatically mean reliable.** Self-written tests can share the implementer’s misunderstanding; an LLM evaluator can share the producer’s biases; retrieved text may be irrelevant or wrong. Reflexion’s feedback-design discussion and the LLM-judge bias results require validation of the feedback channel itself. [Sources](https://arxiv.org/html/2303.11366), [source](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.05685)

- **Independent context can help even without a new model, but it is weaker than new evidence.** CoVe’s factored verification prevents the verification answer from simply attending to and repeating the draft. Its limitations explicitly state that the model’s knowledge and capabilities still bound the result. Use this as a preflight, not as final factual authentication. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.11495)

- **Reasoning in Token Economies: Budget-Aware Evaluation of LLM Reasoning Strategies** (ICML 2024), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.06461). **Evidence type:** budget-aware comparisons of seven reasoning strategies, five datasets and five models, measuring queries, tokens and monetary cost. With comparable compute, chain-of-thought self-consistency frequently outperformed more complex published strategies. Multi-agent debate and Reflexion could worsen with additional budget, unlike self-consistency; dependent sampling can reduce response diversity. **Limitations:** the authors selected representative rather than exhaustive tasks and strategies and explicitly cite time and monetary constraints. **Default: ADOPT** cost-matched evaluation and simple-baseline controls; **AVOID** claiming an orchestration advantage from an uncosted score comparison. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.06461)

- **Structural costs are knowable even without a universal multiplier.** Self-Refine performs an initial generation plus a feedback and a refinement generation per iteration. CoVe performs drafting, verification planning, verification answering and final revision, with factored variants potentially separating verification calls. Reflexion repeats task attempts and adds evaluation, reflection and memory. Multi-agent systems additionally pay for each role’s calls and shared or copied context. These costs follow directly from the published algorithms; none implies a fixed slowdown across implementations. [Sources](https://arxiv.org/html/2303.17651), [source](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.11495), [source](https://arxiv.org/html/2303.11366), [source](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.00352)

- **Token count and query count measure different constraints.** The budget-aware paper evaluates both plus monetary cost. A single long call can exceed several short calls in tokens; many cached calls can have different dollar and latency profiles. Record input tokens, cached input tokens where available, output tokens, calls, tool executions, wall time and dollar cost separately. The final sentence is an operational inference from the paper’s cost model, which distinguishes input and output token prices. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.06461)

- **Default policy by evidence availability:** use deterministic verification first when a specification can be executed; use source-based verification for factual claims; use calibrated human adjudication for ambiguous or consequential judgments; use self-refinement only as a candidate-improvement step; use debate only as an experiment with a cost-matched control. This policy combines the negative self-correction results with the positive tool, feedback and budget results. [Sources](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.01798), [source](https://arxiv.org/html/2210.03629), [source](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.06461)

### Inferences

- **Adopt:** one bounded retry after a new external signal, then escalate or stop. Repeated reflection without new evidence can merely resample correlated errors and consume budget. [Sources](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.01798), [source](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.06461)
- **Test before adopting:** a separate verifier model, diverse sampling, CoVe or multi-agent review. Acceptance requires improvement over the same producer at equal total budget, not merely over one cheap initial answer. [Sources](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.01798), [source](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.06461)
- **Avoid by default:** open-ended debate, automatic acceptance by the author, retrying until a self-critic says “good”, and hiding verifier cost outside the reported budget. The negative and cost-aware studies directly challenge these patterns. [Sources](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.01798), [source](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.06461)
- Report a cost-quality curve rather than one operating point. Include best-of-N or self-consistency, a single-pass baseline and the proposed verifier at matched budgets. Stop adding rounds when marginal verified gain falls below the project’s predefined value or when failure categories remain unchanged. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.06461)

### Gaps

- No cited paper yields a generally optimal retry count, verifier model size or dollar threshold. These are workload and business decisions requiring local measurements.
- The studies do not fully price engineering effort, operational complexity, security exposure, human review time or downstream cost of false acceptance. A complete total-cost comparison must add these locally.
- “Independence” is not a binary property established by using two model names. Shared training data, prompts, retrieval and rubrics can leave correlated errors; the cited papers do not provide a universal correlation metric.

## What do ALCE and Judging LLM-as-a-Judge imply for evidence, citations and adjudication?

### Takeaway

A citation is a checkable claim-to-evidence relationship, not a credibility decoration. Evaluate answer correctness, citation support and citation necessity separately. LLM judges can scale preference evaluation after calibration, but their biases and reasoning limits make them poor unappealable authorities.

### Cited Findings

- **Enabling Large Language Models to Generate Text with Citations** (ALCE, EMNLP 2023), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14627), [ACL record](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.398/), [official code and data](https://github.com/princeton-nlp/ALCE). **Evidence type:** end-to-end retrieval and citation benchmark with automatic metrics and human-evaluation correlations across ASQA, QAMPARI and ELI5. ALCE evaluates fluency, correctness and citation quality; citation recall checks whether statements are supported, while citation precision penalizes unsupported or unnecessary citations. Even the best evaluated models lacked complete citation support 50% of the time on ELI5. **Default: ADOPT** claim-level support and citation-quality checks; **TEST** the automatic implementation on the target domain. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.14627)

- **ALCE’s limitations constrain adjudication.** MAUVE can be sensitive to output length; automatically generated ELI5 claims may omit valid answers; citation quality depends on NLI accuracy; the NLI precision setup cannot properly detect partial support and scored lower than human evaluation in that case. The datasets do not cover all multi-hop reasoning, mathematics or code tasks. **Default: ADOPT** human sampling for partial support, conflicting evidence and high-impact claims; **AVOID** treating NLI entailment as a complete truth judgment. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.14627)

- **Correctness and attribution are separate gates.** ALCE reports prompting configurations with strong correctness but poor citation quality, showing that a plausible or correct answer can still be poorly evidenced. Require each material claim to map to a source passage that actually entails it; check source quality and coverage separately. The final operational rule is an inference from ALCE’s separate metrics. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.14627)

- **Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena** (NeurIPS 2023 Datasets and Benchmarks), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05685), [official proceedings](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/91f18a1287b398d378ef22505bf41832-Abstract-Datasets_and_Benchmarks.html). **Evidence type:** comparison of LLM judgments with controlled expert preferences and crowdsourced human battles. Strong GPT-4 judges achieved over 80% agreement with human preferences in the study, comparable to human-to-human agreement in its setup. The paper identifies position, verbosity and self-enhancement biases plus limited reasoning ability. **Default: TEST** LLM judges for open-ended rubric scoring after human calibration; **AVOID** an uncalibrated judge as the sole release authority. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.05685)

- **Mitigations are procedural, not magical.** The judge paper studies reversing or randomizing answer order for position bias and using references or stronger judging setups where appropriate. Apply blind model identities, fixed rubrics, pairwise order swaps, separation of fluency from correctness, and escalation when swapped judgments disagree. The final list extends the paper’s mitigations into an adjudication protocol. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.05685)

- **Agreement is not identical to correctness.** MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena measure alignment with human preferences in open-ended chat; they do not prove that a judge can verify source entailment, executable behavior or domain-specific safety. ALCE’s metric limitations reinforce the need for task-specific validation. **Default: ADOPT** deterministic checks where available and reserve LLM adjudication for dimensions that genuinely require judgment. [Sources](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.05685), [source](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.14627)

### Inferences

- Use an evidence table with claim, quoted or located source span, source provenance, support status, verifier and unresolved conflict. Mark claims as supported, contradicted, partially supported, unverifiable or out of scope. This makes ALCE-style support auditable and avoids hiding uncertainty behind a fluent citation list. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.14627)
- A verifier should reject missing evidence even when it personally believes the claim. Otherwise the judge substitutes its own parametric knowledge for the requested evidence chain. This follows from separating correctness from citation quality in ALCE. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.14627)
- For adjudication, require a rubric and evidence, not “pick the better answer”. Keep producer and judge contexts separate where leakage could bias the result; escalate order instability, self-preference, partial entailment and consequential disagreements to human review. [Sources](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.05685), [source](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.14627)
- Do not let the same LLM judge be both final evaluator and undisclosed author of the reference answer. The judge paper’s self-enhancement bias and ALCE’s incomplete automatic claim coverage make that a conflicted oracle. [Sources](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.05685), [source](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.14627)

### Gaps

- ALCE’s NLI-based citation metrics require fresh validation for new languages, domains and evidence types. The paper does not justify transferring its human-correlation results unchanged.
- The judge study’s agreement figures are tied to its models, prompts, tasks and human-preference setup. They are not a universal judge accuracy score.
- Neither paper establishes a safe human-review sampling rate for a particular production risk. Set that rate from impact, measured disagreement and audit capacity.

## How should AgentBench, SWE-bench, contamination research and statistical-testing methodology shape acceptance tests?

### Takeaway

Acceptance should combine task-relevant execution, independent regression protection, contamination-resistant holdouts and paired statistical comparison at equal budget. A benchmark score or green test run is evidence, not a complete release decision. Report coverage and failure modes alongside cost and uncertainty.

### Cited Findings

- **AgentBench implies a matrix, not one number.** Its eight environments expose different competencies and its trajectory analysis distinguishes limit-exceeded and other failure causes. Local acceptance should cover each actual workflow class, tool and permission boundary, with per-environment success, failure category, rounds and tokens. The local metrics are an operational inference from AgentBench’s multi-dimensional design. **Default: ADOPT.** [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.03688)

- **SWE-bench: Can Language Models Resolve Real-World GitHub Issues?** (ICLR 2024), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06770), [official project](https://www.swebench.com/). **Evidence type:** execution-based benchmark of 2,294 real GitHub issue and pull-request pairs from 12 popular Python repositories. Given an issue and repository snapshot, the model edits code and repository tests evaluate the patch. In the original baseline evaluation, Claude 2 solved 1.96%, showing the benchmark’s difficulty at that time. **Limitations:** Python-only scope, baseline-specific results and the authors’ explicit warning that execution tests alone do not guarantee comprehensive, efficient or readable solutions. **Default: ADOPT** real issue-to-patch execution tests; **AVOID** interpreting pass rate as complete software quality. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.06770)

- **SWE-bench’s design supports independent acceptance when tests are meaningful.** Keep issue reproduction, candidate patch, existing regression suite and new requirement tests distinct. Record environment setup, test selection and actual executed candidate. Tests inherited from a pull request can encode the requirement, but the paper’s limitation means reviewers must still inspect test sufficiency and solution quality. The procedural rule is an inference from the benchmark design and stated limitation. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.06770)

- **Investigating Data Contamination in Modern Benchmarks for Large Language Models** (NAACL 2024), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09783), [ACL record](https://aclanthology.org/2024.naacl-long.482/). **Evidence type:** retrieval-based overlap analysis for open pretraining corpora plus Testset Slot Guessing for open and proprietary models. TS-Guessing masks a wrong multiple-choice option or an unlikely word and asks for completion. ChatGPT and GPT-4 exactly guessed masked MMLU options at 52% and 57% in the reported setup, raising contamination concerns. **Limitations:** TS-Guessing depends on instruction comprehension; many open models predicted the correct answer regardless of instructions, and multiple-choice overfitting may contribute. Suspicious completion is not by itself proof of exactly how training exposure occurred. **Default: ADOPT** contamination probes as risk evidence, not as a sole verdict. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.09783)

- **Acceptance sets need provenance and renewal.** The contamination paper’s retrieval and probing methods, together with SWE-bench’s continuously extendable issue pipeline, support exact and near-duplicate checks where training data are available, black-box probes where they are not, temporal holdouts and newly collected tasks. Keep a private or rotating acceptance set and report any known overlap. Temporal and private holdouts are operational inferences; no cited method proves a closed model uncontaminated. [Sources](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.09783), [source](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.06770)

- **The Hitchhiker’s Guide to Testing Statistical Significance in Natural Language Processing** (ACL 2018), [paper and metadata](https://aclanthology.org/P18-1128/). **Evidence type:** opinion/theoretical methodology paper, test-selection protocol and survey of ACL/TACL practice. It explains that task, experimental setup and evaluation measure determine the appropriate significance test, and finds significance testing often ignored or misused. **Limitations:** it is not an agent-specific benchmark and predates current LLM APIs; its protocol must be combined with repeated-run designs for nondeterministic agents. **Default: ADOPT** principled test selection instead of declaring victory from a point-estimate difference. [Source](https://aclanthology.org/P18-1128/)

- **Use paired comparisons and report uncertainty.** For two agent variants evaluated on the same tasks, pair outcomes by task and use an appropriate paired randomization or bootstrap procedure; repeat stochastic runs, report sample and run counts, effect sizes and confidence intervals, and correct for multiple comparisons when many metrics or variants are tested. This is a methodology inference from Dror et al.’s test-selection protocol applied to paired agent tasks, not a result measured by that paper. **Default: ADOPT.** [Source](https://aclanthology.org/P18-1128/)

- **Budget is an acceptance dimension.** Compare single-pass, simple sampling/self-consistency and the proposed orchestration at matched query, token and monetary budgets; include latency and tool failures. A quality gain that disappears at equal compute is not evidence for the architecture. **Default: ADOPT.** [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.06461)

- **Proposed acceptance record:** identify requirement and task slice; dataset provenance and contamination check; model, prompt and tool versions; permissions and environment; baseline and equal-budget rule; number of tasks and repeated runs; success and regression results; input/output tokens, calls, wall time and cost; failure categories; citation or judge calibration where used; unresolved coverage gaps. This consolidates AgentBench, SWE-bench, contamination and budget-aware requirements into an auditable unit. [Sources](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.03688), [source](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.06770), [source](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.09783), [source](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.06461)

### Inferences

- **Release gate 1, requirement fidelity:** independent tests or reviewers must establish that the observed behavior satisfies the requirement, not merely that the patch changed behavior. SWE-bench’s execution model supplies a starting oracle, while its limitations require sufficiency review. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.06770)
- **Release gate 2, regression and operational safety:** run relevant existing tests and target integrations, record denied or partial tool actions, and verify stop and escalation behavior. AgentBench’s cross-environment failures show why one successful path cannot stand for the whole tool. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.03688)
- **Release gate 3, evidence quality:** for research or generated documentation, sample claim-level support and adjudicate partial or conflicting evidence; for subjective scoring, validate the judge against humans before relying on it. [Sources](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.14627), [source](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.05685)
- **Release gate 4, reliability and value:** require a predefined minimum verified effect, acceptable confidence interval and acceptable total cost against the strongest simple baseline. Do not cherry-pick the best stochastic run or the best among many uncorrected comparisons. [Sources](https://aclanthology.org/P18-1128/), [source](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.06461)
- Maintain separate coverage figures for static inspection, executed tasks, real integrations and simulated integrations. This is a reporting proposal derived from the different evidence types in AgentBench and SWE-bench; it prevents inventory or benchmark counts from being mislabeled as runtime coverage. [Sources](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.03688), [source](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.06770)

### Gaps

- Public SWE-bench and AgentBench results cannot certify a private codebase, current model version or production integration. Local executable acceptance remains necessary.
- Contamination detection is asymmetric: evidence of overlap or suspicious recall can raise risk, but no cited black-box method can prove absence of contamination in a closed model.
- The statistical methodology source does not prescribe one test for every agent metric. Test choice still depends on independence, pairing, metric distribution, repeated-run hierarchy and the experimental design.
- No source here establishes universal thresholds for acceptable pass rate, latency, cost, human escalation or statistical significance. Those thresholds must be preregistered from product risk and operational capacity.
