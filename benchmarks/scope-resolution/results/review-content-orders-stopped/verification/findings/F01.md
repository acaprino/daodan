# F01: Customer discount is subtracted twice

- Severity: High
- Confidence: 97
- File: `benchmarks/scope-resolution/app/packages/orders/pricing.py`
- Line: 19
- Description and evidence: `net = order.get("net", 0) - compute_discount(order) - compute_discount(order)`, against the docstring at :18 "netto - sconto cliente + IVA (VAT)". With net 100 and 10% loyalty, checkout returns and the ledger stores 97.6 instead of 109.8. Every discounted order is undercharged and its VAT understated, silently.
- Suggested fix: Subtract `compute_discount(order)` once.
- Load-bearing premise: `compute_total` subtracts the discount twice while its contract defines one subtraction.
- Also declared by other reviewers of this finding: the documented order total applies the customer discount exactly once.
- premise_provenance: independent
- Trigger: Any checkout with non-zero `loyalty_percent` and non-zero `net`.
- Counterevidence checked: compute_discount returns the single full discount; no second discount type in orders; volume_discount is shipping only; no test asserts a total.
