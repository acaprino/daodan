# F06: Lockfile contention fails the writer instead of serializing it

- Severity: High
- Confidence: 88
- File: `benchmarks/scope-resolution/app/packages/orders/ledger.py`
- Line: 10
- Description and evidence: `_acquire_lock` is one `os.open(..., O_CREAT | O_EXCL | O_WRONLY)` with no wait or retry, and nothing catches FileExistsError. When two ledger writes overlap, the loser's caller gets FileExistsError about ledger.lock; its order has no row and its stock stays deducted; a refund that collides is not recorded.
- Suggested fix: Make acquisition wait: a kernel-held blocking lock on a permanent file, or O_EXCL retry with backoff up to a deadline and a named timeout error.
- Load-bearing premise: no code path waits for, retries or handles a writer that finds `ledger.lock` already present.
- Also declared by other reviewers of this finding: a writer that finds `ledger.lock` present fails at once, and no caller retries it.
- premise_provenance: independent
- Trigger: Two ledger writes overlap, threads or processes with the same working directory. Concurrent callers are inferred from the package's own locks; no in-scope caller starts threads.
- Counterevidence checked: No except or FileExistsError in app/packages; no docstring declares fail-fast; no outer lock serializes checkout; test_app_structures.py asserts nothing about the ledger's runtime behaviour.
