# F02: Checkout ignores a failed stock reservation

- Severity: High
- Confidence: 95
- File: `benchmarks/scope-resolution/app/packages/orders/service.py`
- Line: 25
- Description and evidence: `inventory.reserve_stock(order["sku"], order["qty"])` discards the boolean whose contract is "False se la scorta non basta" (inventory.py:18). With stock 10 and qty 11, or an unknown sku, no stock moves but the ledger gets a sale line, a receipt is sent and a total returned: goods with no stock are sold.
- Suggested fix: Stop before pricing, ledger and notification when `reserve_stock` is False, with an out-of-stock outcome the caller can read.
- Load-bearing premise: no path in `checkout` reads the result of `reserve_stock`.
- Also declared by other reviewers of this finding: no code path between `reserve_stock` returning False and `ledger.record` stops the checkout. / `checkout` has no path that stops or branches on a `False` from `reserve_stock`.
- premise_provenance: independent
- Trigger: `qty` above available stock, or a sku missing from `STOCK` with `qty > 0`.
- Counterevidence checked: service.py:25 is the only caller of reserve_stock in app/packages; reserve_stock returns False rather than raising; DefaultPipeline.step checks nothing; the dispatcher adds no guard.
- introduced_by_candidate: not applicable on content basis
- evidence_class: derived
