# F14: Price and discount from the payload reach the ledger without type or range check

- Severity: Medium
- Confidence: 70
- File: `benchmarks/scope-resolution/app/packages/orders/pricing.py`
- Line: 14
- Description and evidence: `order.get("net", 0)` and `order.get("loyalty_percent", 0)` are the only inputs of the total. A missing net records 0.0 while stock is debited, indistinguishable from a free order; loyalty 100 or net -100 record -122.0; NaN writes `nan` into the ledger.
- Suggested fix: At entry require `net` as a finite real >= 0 (read `order["net"]` strictly) and `loyalty_percent` in 0-100, excluding bool.
- Load-bearing premise: no code between `Dispatcher.handle` and `ledger.record` constrains `net` or `loyalty_percent`.
- Also declared by other reviewers of this finding: no check on the checkout path rejects an order without `net` before the ledger write.
- premise_provenance: independent
- Trigger: `{"sku":"demo-sku","qty":1}` (no net); `{...,"net":100,"loyalty_percent":100}`; `{...,"net":-100}`; `{...,"net":float("nan")}`.
- Counterevidence checked: No guards in service.py, api.py or pricing.py; no docstring says a missing net means zero; the same `.get(..., 0)` habit in payments/gateway.py could be deliberate tolerance; a trusted upstream would narrow it and none is in scope.
- introduced_by_candidate: not applicable on content basis
- evidence_class: derived
